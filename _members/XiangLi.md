I received my bachelor’s degree in Neuroscience & Behavior, with a minor in Computer Science, from Mount Holyoke College (US) in 2022. In 2024, I completed a master’s in Computational Biology at Weill Cornell Medicine, where I used nanopore sequencing to profile brain tissue at single-cell resolution. I joined the Shi Lab in 2025 as a Ph.D. student, pursuing the development of cutting-edge sequencing technologies to unveal small RNA modifications in a high-throughput manner. Beyond research, I enjoy exploring diverse cuisines and experimenting with new recipes.
