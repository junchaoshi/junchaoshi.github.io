In collaboration with Prof. Ying Zhang’s lab, we moved from the paternal story to the maternal side of intergenerational inheritance. This work has been published in [Nature Communications](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-63054-5). By pinpointing uterine-fluid small RNAs as messengers, we open a window for early risk detection and potential intervention.
