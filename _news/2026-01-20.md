We’re excited to share our new [EMBO Journal](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1038/s44318-025-00687-8) paper! Using PANDORA-seq, we reveal aging-associated shifts in sperm small RNAs, including a conserved rsRNA length shift in sperm heads across mice and humans, and a pronounced “aging cliff” during the mid-life transition.
